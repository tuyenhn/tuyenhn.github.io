Hi! I'm Tuyen Huynh

  • pronunciation: "tween"
  • Vietnamese script and order: Huỳnh Ngọc Tuyên

I am ...

  • a PhD student, registered with the University of Oxford, Nuffield Department of Medicine; part of the Brasenose College (Apr 2023 - Apr 2027)
  • interested in building Machine Learning models and pipelines for infectious disease forecasting, along with stakeholder engagement
  • currently working on building models for dengue forecasting, part of a project called DART - Dengue Advanced Readiness Tools
  • physically based in the Mathematical Modelling group of the Oxford University Clinical Research Unit (OUCRU), Ho Chi Minh City, Viet Nam
  • supervised by Assoc. Prof. Marc Choisy, Prof. Sophie Yacoub, Assoc. Prof. Sarah Sparrow, and Prof. Moritz Kraemer

I have ...

  • a background in Computer Science and Information Technology with a Bachelor in Information Technology at RMIT University Vietnam (2019-2022); Outstanding Graduate of 2023 (top of class)
  • worked as a Research Assistant (Software Developer) in the Mathematical Modelling group at OUCRU (Aug 2022 - Mar 2023)
